# Problem Statement

## The Problem

Finding suitable student accommodation can be time-consuming because students need to evaluate multiple factors such as price, location, room type, amenities, and availability.

Important information may also be presented differently across listings, making it difficult to compare options quickly.

## How Might We?

**How might we help students find and compare suitable housing options quickly while making important information easy to understand?**

## Design Goal

Create a simple housing search experience that reduces the effort required to discover, evaluate, and save potential accommodation.
